## Primary gameplay promise and acquisition roots

The additive promise is physical, species-specific seasonal behavior for eligible **Dynamic Trees** trees under **Serene Seasons**: deciduous leaves shed without seasonal loot, remain absent during dormancy, and return through *one-use*, support- and obstruction-respecting opportunities; cherry and other eligible species have season-specific blossom visuals. Newly world-generated eligible trees are intended to start with season-correct foliage **before first chunk visibility** (not leafy followed by a tick-later strip). Evergreen/unchanged controls retain native foliage; tropical dry-deciduous profiles use their eligible local calendar. The default server limits are 64 mutation attempts and 2,048 candidate/topology inspections per tick; synchronous birth initialization has a separate geometry-derived bound. This is the implementation's player-facing contract (`README.md`, `docs/spring-generation.md`, `SeasonalRuntime`, `SpringBirthSchedule`), **not** a claim that the candidate JAR or friends' world has demonstrated it.

This mod registers **no new obtainable items, blocks, recipes, loot tables, machines or GUI**: `BetterSeasonalTrees` registers the runtime and networking; `src/main/resources/data/better_seasonal_trees/seasonal_trees/*.json` defines species profiles rather than crafting recipes. The acquisition roots are **native Dynamic Trees trees/seeds/saplings and their parent-mod acquisition/world generation**, not a Better Seasonal Trees recipe or creative item. Pilot native fixtures generate DT trees through their species instead of crafting new blocks (`SeasonalPilotGameTests`, `SeasonalSafetyGameTests`). Verify survival access to the relevant trees through pack worldgen and parent-mod recipes/loot; this repo cannot prove pack-wide acquisition. Validate additive autumn/bloom cosmetics against the parent tree's ordinary wood, fruit, seed and leaf drops: seasons must not introduce bonus products or remove native harvest routes. `/seasonaltrees validate`, `inspect <root position>` and `reconcile <root position>` are permission-level-2 operator diagnostics, not player progression or a GUI (`README.md`, `SeasonalCommands`). The 48-profile data roster and optional-mod activation are enumerated in `README.md` and tested syntactically in `CatalogueParserTest`; live registry compatibility is separate.
